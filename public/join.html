How tracking works
  1. Join — enter your name once, above.
  2. Connect — we set up the free Traccar app on this phone with one tap (install it first if you don't have it). It streams GPS to the race clock.
  3. Run — background Traccar, open your agent app, go. Laps count automatically when you complete the course circuit at running pace.

Handovers: nothing to tap, ever. Share one phone or use your own — whichever phone completes a lap scores it (one lap per team at a time, enforced automatically). Keep your phone charged — the board shows your battery so the marshals will nag you.
